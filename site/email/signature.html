Put it in Gmail

  1. In Gmail on a computer, click the gear, then See all settings.
  2. On the General tab, scroll to Signature and click Create new. Give it a name, like SmileMile.
  3. Click in the big box and paste (Ctrl+V, or Cmd+V on a Mac).
  4. Under Signature defaults, pick your SmileMile address in the first dropdown, and choose the new signature for both new emails and replies.
  5. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.
